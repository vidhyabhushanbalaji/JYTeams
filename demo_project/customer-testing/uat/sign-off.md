## Conditions

A conditional sign-off is allowed for Sev-3 defects and P2 exceptions only, and must state:

1. What is not working, in customer terms.
2. The workaround, if any.
3. The owner and the date for the fix.
4. What happens if the date is missed.

A condition without all four items is not accepted. This is the only place in the programme where a
verbal promise has ever been allowed, and it has never ended well.
