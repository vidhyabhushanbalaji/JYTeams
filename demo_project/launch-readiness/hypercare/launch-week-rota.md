# Launch Week Rota

Status: Draft · Owner: Marcus Feld · Last updated: 2026-11-13

Who is on duty for the website launch week, 17–24 May 2027, hour by hour. Named people in specific
slots, because "the team will be available" has never once been true.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| 11:00 | Comms sent: stores, carriers, contact centre | Jo Bell |
| 14:00 | Feed freshness check, all five sources | Dan Whitcombe |
| 16:00 | War room setup and screen check | Nadia Kerr |
| 17:00 | Go-ahead confirmation to the sponsor | Priya Raman |
| 18:00 | On-call handover | Primary engineer |

| Time | Activity | Who |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 07:00 | War room opens, dashboards verified | Nadia Kerr, Marcus Feld |

The 15:00 slot matters more than it looks: the first orders placed after the 12:30 promise cut-off are
the first real test of the cut-off logic under live conditions.
