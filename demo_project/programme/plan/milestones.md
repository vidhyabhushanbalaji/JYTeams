## What "signed off" means

A milestone is complete when the named owner records approval in the steering minutes, not when the
work feels finished. Verbal approval at a stand-up does not count; that rule was added after M1
nearly slipped on an unrecorded agreement.

## Slack in the plan

There is no float between M5 and M6 (11 days, mostly the bank holiday weekend), and none between M7
and M8 (7 days). Those are the two places where a slip becomes a launch slip. Everything else can
absorb a week without moving M7.
