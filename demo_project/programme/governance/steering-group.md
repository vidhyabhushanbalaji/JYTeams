# Steering Group

Status: Agreed · Owner: Priya Raman · Last updated: 2026-11-06

The steering group is the only body that can move a milestone date, approve spend above £25 k, or
authorise a launch. It meets fortnightly on Thursdays at 10:00 for 45 minutes.

## Membership

| Name | Role | Interest |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Rachel Okonjo | Chief Customer Officer (sponsor, chair) | Customer promise and brand risk |
| Iain McCrae | Chief Information Officer | Platform, security, run cost |
| Ruth Iyer | Head of Trading | Channel revenue and promotion calendar |
| Sofia Mendes | Head of Fulfilment | Stock accuracy and warehouse operations |
| Priya Raman | Programme lead (secretary) | Delivery, risk, budget |

Quorum is the chair plus two members. The programme lead does not vote.

## Standing agenda

1. Status and RAG, five minutes, no reading of the report aloud.
2. Decisions requested, listed in the paper with a recommendation.
3. Risks scoring 15 or above, and any new risk raised since the last meeting.
4. Change requests above the programme-board threshold.
5. AOB, strictly five minutes.

## How papers are circulated

The status report and decision paper go out 48 hours before the meeting. Anything arriving later
is deferred to the next meeting, without exception. This rule exists because three separate
decisions in October were made on numbers nobody had time to check.

## Escalation into the group

Workstream leads raise items through the programme lead by Tuesday 17:00. If a lead believes the
programme is being misled about progress, they may raise it directly with the sponsor; that right
has been used once, in October, and is deliberately kept.
